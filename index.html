Listings, booklet photos, Yiddish descriptions and family reviews come from the Navigation Sukkos 5787 guide published at yazory.com. Google's own reviews can't be copied in; the "Google reviews" button opens them on Google Maps. Hours and prices were set at print time for Chol Hamoed; where it says "Checked on their website", a helper compared them with the place's own website on that date. Always call before you go.

The guide doesn't list websites; they were looked up separately and checked against each place's phone number or address where possible. Extra photos come from each place's own website, and photos showing people were left out.
