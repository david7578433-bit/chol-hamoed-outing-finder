Quick answers
How are the drive times worked out?
They are estimates for normal traffic from the community you pick under "From". Chol Hamoed traffic can be heavier, so leave extra time.
Are the hours and prices up to date?
They come from the printed guide. Every morning a checker compares them with each place's own website, and "Checked on their website" shows what it found. Always call before you go.
How do I save places for my trip?
Tap the star on any place. Your list is under My Trips, and it is saved on this phone or computer only.
What can Ask AI do?
Describe your family and your day, and it suggests places from this guide only. It can make mistakes, so check the details before you go.
A place is missing.
The site lists the places in the Navigation Sukkos guide. Send us the name and we'll look at adding it.
